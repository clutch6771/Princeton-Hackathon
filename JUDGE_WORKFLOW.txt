JUDGE WORKFLOW
==============
How the Judge turns uploaded frames and audio into stored, value-relative
verdicts and batched warnings. Code: watcher_Agent/judge.
What it writes and who reads it: JUDGE_MEMORY_OUTPUTS.txt.

The Judge analyses the exposure it observed (sampled frames and the audio it
was allowed to keep). It does not claim to have watched the whole video.


OVERVIEW
--------
  device adapter                      Judge backend (one process)
  --------------                      ---------------------------
  JPEG every 2 s   -> POST /ingest/frame -+
  AAC chunk ~5 s   -> POST /ingest/audio -+-> 1 admit   2 privacy gate
                                              3 segment 4 finalize candidate
  response: ack + notify|null  <------------- 11 dispatch
                                              5 assemble audio (worker)
                                              6 load approved values + examples
                                              7 ONE Gemini call: transcribe,
                                                describe, judge
                                              8 validate + persist (one outcome)
                                              9 embed (async, separate)
                                              10 ordered batch accounting
  POST /session/end  ------------------------> 12 end session, flush tail

Requests return promptly. Steps 5 to 10 run in a bounded worker queue.
A maintenance tick (every 0.5 s) drives everything that depends on time.


STEP BY STEP
------------
1. Admit            judge/ingest/service.py, judge/api/deps.py
   Check the demo identity, size limits (400 kB request) and the metadata
   contract. First valid upload creates the session from the client's UUID
   and binds one capture source. Duplicate event_id -> "duplicate", no work.
   A second source or an ended session -> 409.
   An acknowledgment means "admitted to in-memory processing", not stored.

2. Privacy gate     judge/privacy/eligibility.py
   Keep media only if the native gate says: eligible, Instagram Reels.
   Anything else is dropped at once ("privacy_excluded") and closes the
   current reel. Audio is kept only inside natively certified intervals;
   uncertified chunks are not even decoded. No media reaches a model here.

3. Segment          judge/segmentation, judge/ingest/session_state.py
   A new reel needs a sharp whole-frame change PLUS evidence: a native
   transition hint, a change of the overlay text layout, or a frame gap
   over 8 s. A frame change alone is treated as a cut inside the same reel.
   Also closes on: left the feed, 90 s cap, session end.

4. Finalize         judge/pipeline/finalizer.py
   On close, a "pending" reel row is stored with its capture-order number.
   The candidate then waits for its last permitted audio: until the audio
   watermark passes its window, or 6 s, whichever is first. No request is
   held open for this.

5. Assemble audio   judge/audio, judge/transcriber/prepare.py
   Each AAC chunk was decoded on its own. PCM is cut by capture timestamps
   to the reel's window and joined into one in-memory WAV. The uncertain
   interval between two reels (up to 2 s) goes to neither reel.

6. Load context     judge/values/reader.py, judge/feedback/retriever.py
   Read one immutable snapshot of approved values (read-only) and up to
   three recent explicit feedback examples. Values missing, invalid,
   unapproved, deleted or over the size cap -> wait at most 20 s, then the
   reel is recorded as values_unavailable. Never neutral, never truncated.

7. Understand       judge/understanding
   One Gemini call with 1-3 frames, the audio clip, the values and the
   examples. It returns transcript, on-screen text, description, features,
   verdict (aligned | neutral | conflict), confidence, value ids, reason.
   No separate speech-recognition call, no repair call. One retry only for
   timeouts, 429 and 5xx. Values, feedback, captions and speech are data,
   wrapped in nonce delimiters; the model has no tools.

8. Validate, persist judge/understanding/validation.py, judge/pipeline/completion.py
   Check value ids against the pinned snapshot, confidence range, reason,
   and that the transcript matches the audio evidence. Then commit, in one
   transaction: the outcome, the single thumbnail, the pending-embedding
   marker, batch accounting and any warning.

9. Embed            judge/embedding/indexer.py
   Separately and later: one embedding of description + transcript. A
   failure retries from stored text and never affects the verdict.

10. Account         judge/notifications/accounting.py
    Count judged reels once, in capture order, into non-overlapping batches
    (default 15). Failed reels are counted as "excluded". When a batch is
    full: conflicts >= 3 and no cooldown and nothing pending -> create one
    warning. Otherwise record why not. Suppressed batches are never sent later.

11. Dispatch        judge/notifications/outbox.py
    The next ingest (or session-end) response returns the warning as
    "notify". It is leased for 30 s; without an acknowledgment the same id
    is returned again, at most 3 times, until it expires (120 s).
    Cooldown: 600 s per user, across sessions.

12. End session     judge/pipeline/lifecycle.py
    POST /session/end, or 600 s without an accepted upload. The open reel is
    closed and flushed. When every reel is final the session becomes "ended"
    and one session_completed event is written. Nobody is invoked.


WHAT A REEL CAN END AS
----------------------
  judged                 verdict stored (aligned | neutral | conflict)
  insufficient_evidence  too few frames
  values_unavailable     no usable approved values
  failed_provider        timeout, quota, outage, local rate limit
  failed_validation      model output failed the checks
  failed_timeout         job deadline (30 s) passed
  skipped_overload       queue or memory budget full
  lost_on_restart        process restarted while media was only in RAM
Only "judged" has a verdict. The others have verdict null and an error code,
are excluded from batches, and are still counted as coverage.


TIME AND ORDER
--------------
  Capture clock   aligns frames with audio and reels with each other.
  Server clock    receipt, 600 s idle, 600 s cooldown, leases, deadlines.
  Order           (capture time, sequence). Late frames are acknowledged and
                  dropped; they never move a boundary.
  Latency target  about 10 s from observed reel end to stored verdict,
                  measured per reel (timings, latency_overrun). A target,
                  not a guarantee.


MEMORY
------
Raw frames and audio live only in RAM, inside a fixed budget (256 MB process,
24 MB per session). Over budget -> HTTP 503 and the upload is dropped. Media
is released on success, failure, timeout, cancellation and shutdown. A
restart loses in-flight media; those reels become lost_on_restart.
The service must run as ONE process.


WHAT IS NOT VERIFIED BY THE TESTS
---------------------------------
Real Instagram frames and audio, native capture memory, Gemini's judgment
quality and quotas, and notification banners. See
watcher_Agent/docs/PRD_TRACEABILITY.txt.
